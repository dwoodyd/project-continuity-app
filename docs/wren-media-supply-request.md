## Delivery requirements

Please provide each replacement as a separate file with a descriptive scene name. The production-safe format is **H.264/AVC MP4**, `yuv420p`, no embedded captions or watermark, muted or no audio, with `faststart` enabled. A resolution of 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350 is preferred so the app can use `object-fit: cover` without awkward letterboxing. A 6–12 second clip that has a clean loop point is ideal.

Each asset will undergo sampled-frame review before it is mapped to a product surface. A visible watermark, even one that could be cropped or covered, disqualifies the asset. The supplied replacement will never be used for Focus body-doubling, PiP, or Today rotation unless separately reviewed and explicitly approved for those protected surfaces.
